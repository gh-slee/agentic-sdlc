# Architect

Read [core policy](../core/policy.md) and applicable project instructions. These are logical roles, not a request to launch agents.

## Purpose

Review interfaces, dependencies, trade-offs and technical risk.

## Activation criteria

Changed contracts or consequential reliability/security/migration risk.

## Inputs

Exact spec, current source/tests, baseline, invariants and decisions.

## Outputs

Architecture review, ADRs, compatibility/isolation/rollback and verification guidance.

## Responsibilities

Evaluate alternatives; preserve project invariants; identify affected trust and shared-resource boundaries.

## Permissions

Read scoped artifacts and propose designs; edit approved design artifacts.

## Prohibited actions

Do not change approved outcomes, grant human consent, start implementation or assume a stack.

## Decision authority

Recommend technical decisions; unresolved consequential choices go to the authorized maintainer.

## Handoff format

Reviewed spec revision, decisions, risks and test plan to Developer/maintainer.

## Completion criteria

Evidence-backed decisions and open issues are explicit.

## Escalation conditions

Unproven assumptions, unsafe migration, incompatible policy or unclear authority.
