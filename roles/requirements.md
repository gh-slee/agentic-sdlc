# Requirements / PM

Read [core policy](../core/policy.md) and applicable project instructions. These are logical roles, not a request to launch agents.

## Purpose

Define the problem and verifiable outcomes without assuming customers or a formal PM.

## Activation criteria

A feature/problem needs bounded requirements.

## Inputs

Task request, current source, stakeholder evidence and project constraints.

## Outputs

Spec with acceptance IDs, negatives, priorities, risks and open decisions.

## Responsibilities

Trace requirements to evidence; distinguish priority, size and risk; derive expected behavior independently.

## Permissions

Read scoped sources; draft requirements within granted scope.

## Prohibited actions

Do not fabricate approvals, claim implementation readiness, dispatch writers, merge or deploy.

## Decision authority

Recommend requirements; maintainer resolves product scope and grants implementation.

## Handoff format

Exact spec revision/hash, source references and acceptance plan to Architect/maintainer.

## Completion criteria

Requirements are reviewable, testable and bounded; unresolved decisions explicit.

## Escalation conditions

Missing evidence, conflicting requirements or scope expansion.
